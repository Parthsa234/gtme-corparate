01 Why teams stall

Your reps are doing work a system should do.

Most teams already pay for the tools. What's missing is the system that connects them, and people on the team who can build and run it.

01Research

Hours on research, minutes on conversations

Reps research accounts by hand before every message, so the work that needs a human gets the least time.

02Tools

Tools bought, never wired together

A data tool, a sequencer and a CRM that don't pass information to each other, and seats nobody fully uses.

03Messaging

Every rep writes their own way

No shared copy framework, no segment tests, so nobody can say which message works.

04AI adoption

AI used ad hoc, not as a process

Individuals try AI tools on the side; the team has no agreed way to use them for research and first drafts.

05Visibility

No shared scoreboard

Leaders can't see sent, replies and opportunities by segment each week, so decisions run on gut feel.

06Deliverability

Volume puts the domain at risk

Sending more from the company domain without warmed mailboxes and limits risks the inbox everyone depends on.

Q.01Do our reps need to code?+

No. The machines are built in Clay, Claude and a sequencer. Where a script helps, the team gets the script and we show what it does.

Q.02Can we use the tools we already pay for?+

Often, yes. Many teams already pay for some of the stack. On the call we look at what you have before recommending anything new.

Q.03How is the training delivered?+

Live on Zoom, with your team's work reviewed on screen, never pre-recorded clicks. The exact format for your team is agreed after the consultation.

Q.04Which roles should join?+

SDRs and BDRs who send, whoever owns the sales stack (RevOps or growth), and the sales leader who reads the numbers.

Q.05What does the team keep afterwards?+

The Clay tables, the research-agent prompts, the copy framework and the infrastructure cost sheet, so the system keeps running after the training ends.

Q.06How is it invoiced?+

Indian companies get a GST invoice and can pay by PO. Outside India, we invoice in USD.

Q.07Is this for one person?+

Team training is for two or more people. For yourself, see the individual GTM engineering program.
